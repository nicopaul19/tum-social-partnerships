## Active Learnings

- When the AI use case is speculative, frame it as an example with wording such as "konnte zum Beispiel spannend sein" or "eine Zusammenarbeit konnte beispielsweise", then ask where the organization's real bottlenecks are.
- Keep appreciation short and specific. One concrete sentence is better than broad praise about the organization's importance.
- Avoid overclaiming that TUM Social AI already understands the organization's internal processes. Ask to learn about priorities instead.
- For generic inboxes, make forwarding easy by naming likely internal owners such as IT, Digitalisierung, Innovation, or Projekte.
